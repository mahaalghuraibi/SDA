About Mood Notes

Mood Notes is a personal space where you can write about your day, your mood, and how you feel 💖

Each note can include:

  1. Your daily mood 😊
  2. Short thoughts or reflections 💭
  3. An emoji or color that represents your feeling 🎨
  4. A photo or GIF for the vibe of your day 📸

It’s your safe space to express yourself freely 🌸
